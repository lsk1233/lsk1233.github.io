Researcher · AIR Lab, Dongguk University

I work on autonomous UAV scanning and path planning for large-scale aerial 3D reconstruction. My research focuses on online coverage evaluation and budget-constrained view planning, so that a drone can capture a complete scene in a single flight — without a prior 3D model or costly revisit missions.

I am also interested in vision-based perception for aerial robots, including semantic segmentation of drone imagery.
