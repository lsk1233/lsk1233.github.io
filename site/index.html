IROS 2026 · ConferenceAccepted

Online Explore-Then-Exploit UAV Scanning with Budgeted Return Re-Scanning

Sanggi Lee, Inseong Choi, Soohwan Song†

Completes initial scanning and targeted re-scanning within a single flight. Captured images are processed during outbound flight to detect under-scanned regions, and the return path is optimized with sector-level budgeted routing and ALNS refinement — improving reconstruction completeness under identical travel budgets.

ISIS 2025 · ConferencePublished

Efficient UAV-based 3D Reconstruction through Online Quality Assessment and Adaptive Re-Scanning

Sanggi Lee, Soohwan Song†

An adaptive re-scanning framework that runs real-time multi-view stereo on a ground server during the UAV's initial flight, detects low-quality regions on the fly, and re-scans them on the return trip — reducing incomplete areas from 6.63% to 0.03% within a single flight.

MDPI Electronics 2025 · JournalPublished

Stereo-GS: Online 3D Gaussian Splatting Mapping Using Stereo Depth Estimation

Junkyu Park*, Byeonggwon Lee*, Sanggi Lee, Soohwan Song† (*equal contribution)

A real-time online 3DGS system that reconstructs photorealistic scenes from streaming stereo pairs. Combines stereo DROID-SLAM tracking with FoundationStereo depth, a two-stage depth filtering pipeline, and PSNR-guided Gaussian initialization — state-of-the-art results on EuRoC and TartanAir.
